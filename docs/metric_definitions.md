# Metric Definitions

## DAU
Daily active users. Count of distinct users who started at least one session on a given day.

## WAU
Weekly active users. Count of distinct users active in the last 7 days.

## MAU
Monthly active users. Count of distinct users active in the last 30 days.

## Engaged DAU
Subset of DAU who completed at least one high-intent event such as sending a message, opening a notification, or using a key product feature.

## Message delivery rate
Delivered messages divided by total messages sent.

## Message open rate
Opened messages divided by delivered messages.

## Click-to-open rate
Clicked messages divided by opened messages.

## Notification open rate
Opened notifications divided by notifications sent.

## D1 / D7 / D30 retention
Share of users in a signup cohort who returned after 1, 7, or 30 days.

## Funnel conversion
Share of users moving from one funnel step to the next.

## Feature adoption rate
Distinct users who used a feature divided by total distinct users.

## Crash rate
Crashes divided by total users on the platform or platform segment for the same period.

## Platform-wide risk score
Average risk score from spam or abuse flags, rescaled to a 0-100 view.
